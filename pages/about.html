Our Story

Where the name became a promise

Est. 2020 · Giza, Egypt

A.O.L.S opened its doors with a small idea that never got smaller: that a child could grow up rooted in Egyptian identity while feeling completely at home in the languages of the wider world. We named the school after Ahmed Orabi, a man remembered not for titles but for the plain-spoken dignity with which he stood for his country. That is the standard we hold ourselves to.

What began as a handful of classrooms has grown into a full journey from Kindergarten through Secondary — but the growth was never the point. Every stage we added was added because a family asked for somewhere they would not have to choose between excellence and warmth.

Today, A.O.L.S exists for the same reason it always has: to hand our students two things at once — fluency in the languages that will carry them anywhere, and a sense of who they are that travels with them wherever they go.
